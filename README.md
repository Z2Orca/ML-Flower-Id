# ML-Flower-Id
Identifies 5 different types of flowers


Looking back at this unit from SparkNC, the coolest part was just seeing how AI actually learns from data, even though it was a bit confusing at first to learn how a computer can make decisions on its own. I managed to get through the harder things by just slowing down during the tests. I see a lot of capability for this and how easy it is to create. Seeing how fast this stuff is growing makes me worry about ethical issues like privacy. If the data we feed into these models is flawed or biased information we receive could be ethically incorrect or wrong.
